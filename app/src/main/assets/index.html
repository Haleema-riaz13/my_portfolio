GoLahore App

GoLahore is my Final Year Project and the strongest project in my portfolio. It is a Lahore-based smart transport app that helps users search routes, compare public/private/hybrid travel options, and choose the best way to reach their destination.

In this project, I worked from base to final output: first I designed the complete UI/UX in Figma, then I converted the design into a Flutter mobile app frontend. I focused on clean navigation, modern cards, route screens, booking screens, driver flow, safety screens, and a user-friendly transport experience.

The app includes public transport route planning, private ride booking, hybrid route suggestions, driver mode, offline-first functionality, and ML-based fare prediction — built on a full Firebase and REST API backend. It was successfully demonstrated as a fully functional application at UCP Open House 2026.
