GoLahore App

GoLahore is my Final Year Project and the flagship of my app-development portfolio. It is a Lahore-based smart transport app that helps users search routes, compare public/private/hybrid travel options, and choose the best way to reach their destination.

I designed the complete UI/UX in Figma, then built the Flutter frontend myself — route screens, booking screens, driver flow, safety screens — on top of a Firebase and REST API backend, with offline-first support and ML-based fare prediction. It was demonstrated as a fully functional app at UCP Open House 2026.
